# 09 UI/UX Audit

## Scope reviewed
The UI/UX audit reviewed the mobile screens in [mobile/lib/screens](mobile/lib/screens), the dashboard pages in [rescue-dashboard/src/pages](rescue-dashboard/src/pages), and the general app shell in [mobile/lib/main.dart](mobile/lib/main.dart).

## Strengths
- The mobile app puts safety and emergency actions at the center of the experience.
- The dashboard has a polished, modern interface that is likely to be effective for operators.
- The use of clear cards, status panels, and route-oriented navigation is appropriate for the product domain.

## Concerns
- The product still appears to be more functionally complete than human-factor refined.
- A field product for fishermen should be tested under real conditions such as low-light, wet hands, noise, and limited connectivity.
- More accessibility, keyboard, and usability hardening would be appropriate before broad deployment.

## UI/UX verdict
- UI/UX readiness: approximately 73%.
- Status: good foundation, but field-validation and operational usability refinement are still needed.
